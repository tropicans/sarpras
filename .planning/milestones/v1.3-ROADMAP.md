- [x] **Phase 8: Dual-Channel Notification Integration (Resend Email + Fonnte WhatsApp)** (2/2 plans) — completed 2026-08-14
  - **Goal**: Implement Resend email integration, responsive email templates, unified dual-channel async dispatches (Email + WhatsApp), and multi-channel audit logging.
  - **Depends on**: Phase 7
  - **Requirements**: EMAIL-01, EMAIL-02, EMAIL-03, EMAIL-04, EMAIL-05, EMAIL-06, EMAIL-07, EMAIL-08, NOTIF-01, NOTIF-02
  - **Success Criteria**:
    1. Resend client adapter and mock/logger fallback correctly format, validate, and dispatch email payloads.
    2. Responsive HTML & plaintext email templates render Indonesian notifications for booking submission, admin alerts, approval, and rejection with accurate WIB wall-clock times and tracking links.
    3. `BookingService` lifecycle hooks trigger concurrent, non-blocking asynchronous dispatches for both Email and WhatsApp without transaction latency penalties.
    4. Notification audit log records both channel types with delivery status, recipient target, reference codes, and timestamps.
    5. Automated test suite verifies email templates, mock gateway behaviors, dual-channel dispatch mechanics, and error resilience.
