## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone 1.1 has achieved full role-based segregation of duties across frontend routing, user interface controls, and backend server-boundary functions:
- **Admin Role** has exclusive access to User Management (`/admin/users`) and Audit Logs (`/admin/audit`).
- **Operator Role** has complete operational access to Asset Management (`/admin/assets`) and Bookings (`/admin/bookings`), but is restricted from User Management and Audit Logs.
- **Pimpinan Role** has a strict read-only view of the Dashboard, Calendar, Bookings, and Assets. All modification actions, including creating/editing assets, setting schedules, and approving/rejecting/cancelling bookings are hidden from the UI and protected on the backend.

## 3. Integration & Flow Verification

### Flow 1: Administrator Operations
1. Administrator signs in and has full access to the sidebar navigation.
2. Direct loading of `/admin/users` and `/admin/audit` routes succeeds.
3. Invocation of `getAdminsListFn` and `getAdminAuditLogsFn` returns valid lists.

### Flow 2: Operator Operations
1. Operator signs in. Sidebar navigation conditionally hides "Manajemen Pengguna" and "Riwayat Audit" links.
2. Attempting to manually navigate to `/admin/users` or `/admin/audit` triggers the route's `beforeLoad` check, throwing a redirect to `/admin`.
3. Operator can view, create, and update assets, and approve or reject booking requests.

### Flow 3: Pimpinan (View-Only) Monitoring
1. Pimpinan signs in. Sidebar navigation hides admin-only routes.
2. Pimpinan can load `/admin/assets` and `/admin/bookings`.
3. In `/admin/assets`, the "Tambah Aset" button and inline action buttons (Calendar, Edit, Archive) are hidden.
4. In `/admin/bookings`, the inline "Setujui" and "Tolak" buttons are hidden.
5. In the Booking Review Drawer, the action footer containing "Tolak Permohonan" and "Setujui Permohonan" is hidden.
6. Direct backend call attempts to `saveAssetFn` or `approveBookingAdminFn` fail with a `Forbidden` rank-check error.
