  <files>src/lib/notifications/types.ts, src/lib/notifications/service.server.ts, src/lib/notifications/service.test.ts</files>
  <read_first>src/lib/email/service.server.ts, src/lib/email/templates.ts, src/lib/whatsapp/service.server.ts, src/lib/whatsapp/templates.ts, .planning/phases/08-dual-channel-notification-integration/08-CONTEXT.md, .planning/phases/08-dual-channel-notification-integration/08-RESEARCH.md</read_first>
  <action>
1. Create `src/lib/notifications/types.ts` defining:
   - Event notification payload interfaces: `BookingCreatedNotificationData`, `BookingApprovedNotificationData`, `BookingRejectedNotificationData`, `BookingCancelledNotificationData`.
   - Dispatch summary interface: `DualChannelDispatchSummary`: `{ emailResults: EmailDispatchResult[]; whatsappResults: WhatsAppDispatchResult[] }`.
2. Create `src/lib/notifications/service.server.ts` with unified dispatch functions:
   - `dispatchBookingCreatedNotifications(data)`:
     - Dispatches Email to requester (if email present) with `buildBookingSubmissionRequesterEmail`.
     - Dispatches WhatsApp to requester (if phone present) with `buildBookingSubmissionRequesterMessage`.
     - Dispatches Email to admins for each address in `ADMIN_DEFAULT_EMAIL` (default `"admin@ppkasn.go.id"`) with `buildBookingSubmissionAdminEmail`.
     - Dispatches WhatsApp to admin (if `FONNTE_ADMIN_TARGET` configured) with `buildBookingSubmissionAdminMessage`.
     - Uses `Promise.allSettled` for concurrent non-blocking execution.
   - `dispatchBookingApprovedNotifications(data)`:
     - Dispatches Email to requester (if email present) with `buildBookingApprovalEmail`.
     - Dispatches WhatsApp to requester (if phone present) with `buildBookingApprovalMessage`.
     - Concurrently executed via `Promise.allSettled`.
   - `dispatchBookingRejectedNotifications(data)`:
     - Dispatches Email to requester (if email present) with `buildBookingRejectionEmail` containing `rejectionReason`.
     - Dispatches WhatsApp to requester (if phone present) with `buildBookingRejectionMessage` containing `rejectionReason`.
     - Concurrently executed via `Promise.allSettled`.
   - `dispatchBookingCancelledNotifications(data)`:
     - Dispatches Email to requester (if email present) with `buildBookingCancellationEmail`.
     - Dispatches WhatsApp to requester (if phone present) with `buildBookingCancellationMessage`.
   - `safeDispatchBookingNotifications(fn)`: Catch-all safe invocation wrapper that logs errors without throwing unhandled exceptions.
3. Create `src/lib/notifications/service.test.ts` verifying:
   - Dual dispatch when both phone and email exist.
   - Single dispatch when only email or only phone exists.
   - Graceful skip when neither exists.
   - Admin multi-recipient email dispatches.
   - Gateway fault isolation (email error does not prevent WhatsApp and vice versa).
  </action>
  <verify>node --import tsx --test src/lib/notifications/service.test.ts</verify>

  </acceptance_criteria>
  <done>Unified notification orchestrator delivers concurrent, fault-isolated multi-channel dispatches and passes all tests.</done>
